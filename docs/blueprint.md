# **App Name**: ELF Portal

## Core Features:

- Editorial Multi-Page Experience: Seamless navigation between high-design pages for Programs, The Gauntlet, Impact, and Gallery using fixed navbar with gold-toned status highlights.
- AI Path Recommendation Tool: An intelligent tool that analyzes a user's academic and leadership goals to recommend specific ELF pillars and mentorship paths.
- The Preclinical Gauntlet Dashboard: A high-prestige section dedicated to competition stats, chip-tagged highlights, and direct external gallery integration for flagship event moments.
- Membership Interest Hub: A comprehensive membership portal including the 'Join Us' multi-field form designed to capture future healthcare leaders' metadata.
- Live Impact Analytics: Interactive counter cards and milestone visualizers that showcase quantitative reach across institutions and students.
- Mosaic Community Grid: A magazine-style member spotlight grid displaying dynamic testimonial cards with academic institutional identification.
- Advanced Image Gallery Engine: A dark-mode masonry layout gallery featuring hover-triggered gold overlays and category filtering for diverse organizational events.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep forest green (#0D2B1A) providing a backdrop of prestige and academic gravity.
- Accent color: Warm gold (#C89B3C) for CTAs and highlighted headings, symbolizing 'Investing in Visionaries'.
- Background color: Cream (#FAF7F0) for body sections to ensure high readability and a high-end editorial aesthetic.
- Headline font: 'Cormorant Garamond' (serif) used for bold, italic, and display headings to convey tradition and excellence. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Body font: 'DM Sans' (sans-serif) in weights 300-400 for a clean, accessible reading experience. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Editorial layout featuring generous whitespace, 2-column card grids for pillars, and fixed-position blurred headers.
- Subtle section entries using fade-ins and vertical translation (20px) to enhance the sense of discovery while scrolling.
